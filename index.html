SECOND PREVIEW · ESP32-CAM

What the camera sees

The ESP32-CAM is AquaBot's eyes. It captures a picture of what floats ahead, a trained model classifies it, the logic decides whether to collect, and a one-letter command goes to the Arduino Uno over a single wire.

    FAQ

    Questions, answered

    How does AquaBot find waste?

    An HC-SR04 ultrasonic sensor pings the water ahead and times the echo (distance = echo time ÷ 58 µs per cm). A short echo means an object is floating in front, so the Uno steers toward it.

    Can waste really become oxygen?

    Not directly. Oxygen is released from water by electrolysis or by algae photosynthesis. The mini factory turns waste into usable energy or nutrients that support that process.

    Why is the tunnel underground?

    It keeps the conveyor protected from weather and keeps the shoreline clear, so the dock stays small and the factory can sit safely on land.

    How does the bot find its dock?

    An IR sensor looks for a beacon on the dock. When the signal appears (pin D13 goes LOW), the bot stops, opens its gate and dumps the load into the hopper.

    Why use relays?

    The Uno’s pins give only a few milliamps. A relay lets that tiny signal switch a high-current motor or pump safely, with the power supply kept separate.

    Is it safe to build?

    Keep electronics waterproof, fuse the battery, vent any gas from the O₂ unit (electrolysis also makes hydrogen), and never use a sealed container or sparks near it.
